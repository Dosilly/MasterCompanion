# Fenes clue and handout redesign

6 October 2026

## Player discovery

The existing locations remain Y9 (proof of a successful recovery), Y15 (a first
real voice exchange), and Y19q (portable, sustained contact and a future shared
search). The historical accident is an analogy for Fenes, not an account of her
companions. The first companion she chooses remains alive and available.

Three short in-world handouts replace the long report, correspondence bundle,
and manual. Lethra's library report states the successful return immediately.
Orren's observatory note puts activation before the personal record. The portable
manual stands alone for groups that missed the earlier locations. Personal
interruptions belong to the authors and do not conceal the actionable clues.

Lethra is proud of her work, impatient with inaccurate copying and council
interference, and bound by her promise to release the accountant. Orren is a
patient inventor writing for the next operator; he remembers the people behind
the readings. The accountant is exhausted but stays to help his companion.
The surveyor acts independently and prepares her own end of the return.
Iriolarthas takes control of the successful discovery and access to it.

## Scope and rules

The measurement worksheet is removed completely, including its module material,
reference-fixture entry, location links, and campaign copy. There is no required
replacement form.

The mandatory two-point/two-reading protocol is removed. Initial conversation
establishes safety, situation, and the next attempt. Both people then seek local
knowledge and usable crossings. Route-specific measurements, resources, and
opening work belong to the later adventure once the other world is defined.
The instrument remains a communication and investigation tool, not a compass
or a portal. Completing the Ythryn arc does not require rescuing the companions.

Existing preparation, communication duration, daily limits, voluntary responses,
trace plates, portability, and temporary magical suppression remain unchanged.
Time differences do not permit rewriting past conversations. Silence is not
proof of death; communication and stored traces do not expire during a pause.
