staff lead, and acquisition of the staff with caregiver instructions in Y19f.

returned the staff after twelve years, after her father's death from old age.

protection. The patient is an ancient case, not Harkan's father.

attunement, the five-foot radius, interruption and recovery times, one recipient,
independent magic, and caregiver assistance. The GM guide records the character
voices and document history; Y24's discovery summary reflects the new evidence.
