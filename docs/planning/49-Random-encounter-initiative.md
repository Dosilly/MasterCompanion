# Random encounter initiative variants

7 October 2026

All eleven encounter introductions now distinguish two starting situations.
The combat version begins when an undetected hostile creature starts attacking;
the early detection version pauses before the creatures detect the party and
allows hiding, bypassing, retreating or deliberate contact. Detection follows the
actual scene and creature statistics. Situational initiative does not grant a
bonus to initiative rolls, automatic surprise or free attacks.

Encounter counts, stable material and section IDs, arrival conditions and faction
resources remain unchanged. Iriolarthas attacks only recognized looters, and a
patrol that knows Avarice's truce still respects it. The aggressive nothic opening
is an authored alternative to its ordinary request for food. Magen and living-hand
combat openings begin after the party has entered the controlled area.
